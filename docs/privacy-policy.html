Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2025

Trakt ("we", "our", or "us") operates a CRM platform that helps service professionals track leads originating from Instagram and WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram or WhatsApp Business account to Trakt, we may collect:

  • Instagram user IDs of people who send you direct messages
  • Message content from Instagram DMs received through the Meta Webhooks API
  • Advertising referral data (ad ID, campaign name) when a lead originates from a Meta ad
  • WhatsApp phone numbers and message content when the WhatsApp Business integration is enabled
  • Your Meta access tokens (stored encrypted, used only to communicate with Meta APIs on your behalf)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and update lead profiles in your CRM dashboard
  • To classify message intent and urgency using an AI model (Groq / LLaMA)
  • To attribute leads to the correct advertising campaign or organic source
  • To display analytics and reports in your account

We do not sell your data or your leads' data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following sub-processors, strictly to operate the service:

  • Groq, Inc. — AI inference for message classification (message text is sent for analysis; no data is retained by Groq beyond the API call)
  • MongoDB Atlas (MongoDB, Inc.) — Cloud database hosting for lead and message storage
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — We communicate with the Instagram Graph API and WhatsApp Business API on your behalf
  • Railway Corp. — Cloud infrastructure provider hosting the Trakt backend

4. Data Retention

Lead and message data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access tokens are stored encrypted. We apply the principle of least privilege to all internal services and third-party integrations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your leads
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time by disconnecting your account

7. Children's Privacy

Trakt is a business tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify account holders of material changes via email. Continued use of the service after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
